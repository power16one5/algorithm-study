### 분산
$$
\text {Var}(X) = E((X - E(X))^2) = E(X^2) - (E(X))^2 \\[1em]
$$

분산은 평균과는 다르게 선형적이지 않습니다.

$$
\text {Var}(X + c) = E((X + c - E(X + c))^2) \\[1em]
= E((X + c - E(X) - E(c))^2) \\[1em]
= E((X - E(X))^2) \\[1em]
= \text {Var}(X) \\[3em]
\text {Var}(cX) = E((cX - E(cX))^2) \\[1em]
= E((cX - cE(X))^2) \\[1em]
= E((c(X - E(X)))^2) \\[1em]
= E(c^2(X - E(X))^2) \\[1em]
= c^2 E((X - E(X))^2) \\[1em]
= c^2 \text {Var}(X) \\[1em]
$$

### 정규분포(이어서)
일반적인 정규분포

$$
X = \mu + \sigma Z,\kern{1em} \mu \in \R \kern{0.5em} \text {(mean, location)},\kern{1em} \sigma > 0 \kern{0.5em} \text {(standard deviation, scale)} \\
E(X) = \mu, \kern{0.5em} \text {Var}(\mu + \sigma Z)
$$
